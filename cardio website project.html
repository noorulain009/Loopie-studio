FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services and booking process.

How do I book an appointment?

You can book an appointment by contacting our clinic or using our online booking system.

What heart conditions do you treat?

We treat a comprehensive range of cardiovascular conditions including coronary artery disease, heart failure, arrhythmias, hypertension, valvular heart disease, peripheral artery disease, and more. Our specialists are experienced in both common and complex cardiac conditions.

Do you offer emergency consultations?

Yes, we provide emergency consultations for urgent cardiovascular concerns. Please contact our emergency service for immediate assistance.

What insurance plans are accepted?

We accept a variety of insurance plans. Please contact our office to confirm whether your insurance provider is accepted.

How long does a first consultation take?

A first consultation usually takes around 30 to 60 minutes depending on your condition and medical history.

Do you offer telemedicine consultations?

Yes, telemedicine consultations are available for suitable patients and appointments.
